# LLM Reference Data

## Business Context
- Jaipur Living is a manufacturer and distributor of premium rugs, pillows and decor
- Operating in the United States with a small amount of sales internationally to Canada, Mexico and South America
- B2B Sales to interior designers and home space retailers
- Also sell through online marketplaces such as Amazon, Wayfair, and Rugs Direct, as well as big box retailers like Home Depot, Lowe's, and Target
- US Based To-the-trade only sister company of the largest maker of Handmade Rugs in India

## Business Model
- Trade only sales to interior designers and boutique retailers
- Customers must have an account set up in order to purchase
- B2B sales that are primarily tax-free, for resale & eventual delivery to a third party client (The customer of our customers)
- Majority of sales volume is made through our website (www.jaipurliving.com)- Roughly 40% of total volume, with additional channels making up the rest: Big Box Retail and National Accounts 35% of total volume, and online marketplaces making up the remaining 25%
- Direct fulfillment from our warehouse in Acworth, GA north of Atlanta
- Shipping is via parcel (UPS or FedEx) or LTL for rugs larger than 113" wide
- Customer acquisition via Social Media, Paid Search, Print Media, and word of mouth

## Constraints or special considerations
- B2B Sales only, no D2C which can makes acquisition tricky due to social channels prominence in discovery
- Currently mid digital transformation- moving from a traditional B2B sales rep led sales model to digital first
- Customer base is moving to younger demographic- key challenge is to meet their needs and expectations with a business foundation based on traditional sales and our existing tech stack

## Brand Voice
- Elevated, aspirational brand voice with a luxury presentation 
- Quality products with a compelling social message and heartwarming back story
- Proper, educated language. No contractions, exclamation points, or Emojis in brand communication or copy

## Tech Stack
- Web Platform: Magento / Adobe Commerce Cloud - 2.4.5 (Currently in process of upgrading to 2.4.8)
- ERP: Microsoft Dynamics D365
- CRM: Salesforce
- Tax Software: Avalara
- Web and Marketing Integrations: Klaviyo
- PIM: InRiver- Adobe Commerce Integration for product data & Image sync.
